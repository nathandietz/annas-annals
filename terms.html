These terms apply to your use of Anna’s Annals, a browser-based book catalog that stores your library in a Google Sheet. By using the app, you agree to these terms.

Your library and permissions

Connect only spreadsheets you have permission to use. You are responsible for the information you enter or import and for managing access to your spreadsheets. If you use a shared Sheet, people with edit access can change the catalog. By connecting a Sheet and taking actions in the app, you authorize the app to read the Sheet and apply the changes you request.

Use Anna’s Annals lawfully and respect others’ privacy and access rights. You can stop using the app at any time, disconnect your library, and revoke access in your Google Account. Your Google Sheet remains available through Google according to its sharing settings.

The app relies on services including Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Picker, and Open Library. Your use of those services is subject to their own terms and policies. Book details and covers come from third-party catalogs and may be incomplete or incorrect. Check suggested book details before saving them.

Anna’s Annals is provided as is and as available. To the extent permitted by applicable law, we make no warranties about accuracy, availability, or suitability for a particular purpose. Keep a backup of important catalog data. You can copy your spreadsheet in Google Drive or export your catalog as a CSV from the app.
