About Anna’s Annals

Catalog your books in a Google Sheet you control. Scan ISBN barcodes to look up book details, search your collection, check for duplicates, and import or export your catalog as CSV.

Connect an existing library spreadsheet or create a new one in your Google account. Anna’s Annals asks for per-file permission so it can read the catalog and save your changes in the spreadsheet you select or create. Your Google sharing settings control who can view or edit your library.
