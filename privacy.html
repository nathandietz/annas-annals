Anna’s Annals Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Anna’s Annals is a browser-based book catalog that uses a Google Sheet you select or create to store your library.

Google user data we access

When you connect your Google account, Anna’s Annals receives a time-limited access token from Google under the drive.file permission. Google Picker returns the ID of the spreadsheet you select. The app checks your permission to edit that spreadsheet and reads its title, column headers, and catalog rows from the Books tab, including any extra columns in the range the app reads. Catalog entries can contain titles, authors, ISBNs, shelf locations, notes, and record IDs and timestamps. The app does not request your Google account email or profile or ask for access to all of Drive.

How we use Google user data

The app uses the token to call Google Drive and Google Sheets on your behalf. It uses the spreadsheet ID to reopen your library and your edit permission to determine whether changes can be saved. It uses your catalog to display, search, and check for duplicate books and to generate CSV exports you request. When you create a library or add, edit, import, or delete entries, the app sends those changes to Google Sheets.

Anna’s Annals uses Google user data to provide the library features described here. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and service providers

Google receives authorization requests and the data needed to open, create, or update your spreadsheet. People with access to a shared spreadsheet can see its contents according to its Google sharing settings. Exporting a CSV creates a copy on your device that you control.

For ISBN lookups, your browser sends the ISBN to Open Library and, when configured, Google Books to retrieve book details. If you enable covers, your browser sends ISBNs from your catalog to Open Library to fetch cover images. These lookup and cover requests carry no Google access token, spreadsheet ID, or catalog notes. Camera frames, photos selected for scanning, and CSV files selected for import are processed in your browser. Saving imported or scanned book details sends the resulting entries to Google Sheets.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Your browser also connects to Google for sign-in, APIs, and fonts and to jsDelivr to load the barcode scanner. These providers receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, when your browser contacts them.

Storage, retention, and protection

Anna’s Annals runs in your browser and has no separate server for storing your catalog or token. Requests to Google use HTTPS. Google checks the access token and applies its own file permissions. Your catalog data remains in Google Sheets until you or someone with appropriate permission removes it, subject to Google’s retention and recovery settings.

Your browser’s local storage keeps your selected spreadsheet ID and display preferences so they are available when you return. It also stores the access token with its expiration time. The app discards an expired token when it checks the saved session. The browser’s session storage caches a catalog snapshot for the current tab. If you enable covers, your browser can cache cover images until they are evicted, you clear cached covers in the app, or you clear the site’s data.

Disconnecting and deleting data

In the app, open Settings → Disconnect to clear the saved token, remembered spreadsheet, and catalog cache for the current tab. You can also remove cached covers in Settings or clear the site’s data using your browser. To revoke Google authorization, remove Anna’s Annals from your Google Account connections.

To remove catalog data stored with Google, delete the relevant entries in the app or delete the spreadsheet in Google Drive if you own it. Disconnecting leaves the spreadsheet in your Drive. Delete any CSV copies separately if you no longer need them.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support email shown on the Anna’s Annals Google consent screen.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Anna’s Annals uses Google user data, we will update this page, notify users in the app, and request consent before using Google user data in a new way.
